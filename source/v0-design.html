Handling rules

What to do when restricted content is found

The portfolio review process must stop, isolate, sanitize, and review before anything is moved, reused, or shown.

1Stop portfolio onboarding for that asset.
2Mark it as restricted.
3Remove it from the working set.
4Create a sanitized replacement if needed.
5Request review from the portfolio owner and appropriate UST governance owner.
6Do not publish, demo, or reuse until approved.
